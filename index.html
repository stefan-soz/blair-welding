About Aaron & Blair Welding & Fab

No Shortcuts. No Excuses. Just Hard Work.

My name is Aaron Blair, and I’m the owner and operator of Blair Welding & Fab.

I started Blair Welding & Fab because I wanted to take the experience and skills I’ve gained over the years and build something of my own. I’ve spent a lot of time working in the field, around heavy equipment, structural steel, pipe, and pipeline work. Along the way, I realized there was a need for a dependable welder who would come to the job instead of making people haul their equipment somewhere else.

That’s why I decided to bring mobile welding and fabrication straight to the customer.

From equipment repairs and structural welding to pipe work, pipeline welding, custom fabrication, emergency repairs, and general welding, my goal is simple: show up, find a solution, and do the job right.

I’m not trying to be the biggest welding company out there. I’m focused on building a company people can trust, depend on, and call when they need the job done right.

Every job matters, whether it’s a small repair or a major project. I take pride in my work, stand behind what I build, and appreciate every customer who gives Blair Welding & Fab the opportunity to earn their business.

This is just the beginning, and I’m looking forward to building this company one job at a time.
